# Safety

This is experimental software for a low-cost educational robot arm. It is not a certified industrial motion controller or emergency-stop system.

- Keep the workspace clear before enabling torque.
- Begin with small movements and low speeds.
- Keep an accessible physical power disconnect.
- Do not rely on host software as a certified emergency stop.
- Never move automatically during construction or connection.
- Treat the default joint limits as provisional until verified on the exact hardware.
- Validate calibration and joint direction before running trajectories.
